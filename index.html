YouTube Autopilot

A personal tool for publishing videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

YouTube Autopilot is a private desktop program that runs on its owner's computer. Each day it takes a video that the owner has prepared and uploads it to the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It also adds the video to a playlist and can list or remove the channel's own videos.

The program is not offered to other people and has no user accounts. It works only with the channel of the person who signed in.

Privacy policy

Личная программа автора: каждый день загружает подготовленное видео на собственный YouTube-канал автора через YouTube Data API. Другим людям не предоставляется. Политика конфиденциальности.
